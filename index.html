Introducing Caitlin Clift

Caitlin Clift is a New York-based political science and public policy alum from the University of California Berkeley. She currently works as a freelance reporter while pursuing a masters degree in journalism at CUNY. She specializes in data visualization and labor reporting, honouring the ill-starred Soviet space dogs in her free time.

Top Three Favorite Stories:

  1. On Keeping A Notebook

    • Joan Didion enriches perspective on writing and diaries in this inspiring essay included in her book, Slouching Towards Bethlehem.

  2. The Porn Addicts Who Tried to Rescue the Camgirls

    • Ezra Marcus is a riveting features writer for New York Magazine. I love his relevancy and spunk in each story.

  3. The Kandy-Kolored Tangerine-Flake Streamline Baby

    • This classic New Journalism story by Tom Wolfe, which originally appeared in Esquire, includes innovative writing that accelarated the movement of stream-of-conscious non-fiction narrative!
